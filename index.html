About Me

Space Data Science Intern / B. Tech (Hons.) Candidate in Computer Science and Engineering
HEX20 / Christ University

I'm a research-oriented Computer Science and Engineering student with hands-on experience in image processing, remote sensing, computer vision, and machine learning. I'm primarily interested in image perception and learning-based methods for reconstructing, interpreting, and extracting meaningful information from visual data.

At HEX20 I work on satellite imagery — generating synthetic NIR bands with U-Net to model water quality on Sentinel-2 data, pulling datasets from NASA, Copernicus, and Google Earth Engine, and building ETL pipelines for feature extraction. Earlier I interned on Azure AI at Edunet Foundation and on AWS cloud infrastructure at Acmegrade.

My published work covers real-time safety monitoring for construction sites (IEEE ICAET-2025), and my projects range from ESRGAN-based super resolution for satellite images to a blockchain-based research-gap resolution DApp that won the Sustainability Track at IIT Bombay Techfest 2025.
